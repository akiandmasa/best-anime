# My Anime
 
Some anime just entertain you. A few of them get into you in a way you can't really explain. This page is me trying to explain it anyway.
 
**Site:** https://akiandmasa.github.io/best-anime/
 
## What I want you to understand
 
The Angel Next Door Spoils Me Rotten and Horimiya hit different. I teared up, and they really changed my view. I'm still working out how, and I can't fully put it into words. I'm not trying to get anything out of this. I just want you to understand what it felt like, and maybe feel it too if you watch them.
